## Flow review

1. **Initial arrival — accepted direction.** The staged, image-only arrival is deliberate. The visible brand and contact action remain available. Preserve this owner requirement.
2. **Hero reveal — healthy completed state; intermediate opacity is a design choice.** At 0.8–1 the supplied desktop/tablet heroes are readable and the sticky header does not overlap the hero copy. The 0.3 state intentionally has faint secondary headline text. A live pacing review can assess how long that state lasts, but it is not treated as a defect against the required staged reveal.
3. **Phone hero — Two and Three repaired.** Two’s quiet pale region now keeps headline, supporting copy, and CTA completely clear of the glass edges. The artwork begins below the action and remains recognizable. One’s partial ball crop is visually intentional and preserves a good dark text area. Three’s latest capture moves the supporting sentence and action below the cream court line. Both now sit on uninterrupted blue, with clear separation from the ball and headline. The previously flagged image/text crossing is resolved. No observed horizontal text overflow at 390 px.
4. **Approach — Two now has a distinct visual structure.** On desktop/tablet, the held sculpture with the role-index caption provides a persistent focal point beside sequential responsibilities. This is a meaningful improvement over the earlier empty left field. Phone passages are legible and cleanly stacked, with generous rather than broken spacing. Rows remain tall for their short copy; tightening them slightly is optional polish.
5. **Visibility — One grouping improved; Three’s state emphasis is clear.** One’s inset panel, vertical boundary, and list treatment visibly distinguish this section from ordinary paragraphs. Its phone list wraps long entries cleanly. Three’s large “Visible.” creates a strong state cue; on phone it fits with comfortable side margins. It remains another large typographic element in a text-heavy page, but it now has a specific purpose. The alternate state word is “Private.”; tab interactions still need functional evidence beyond these default-tab screenshots.
6. **Licensing — orderly and readable.** Desktop alternatives compare cleanly; phone stacking showed no collision in the inspected capture. The fee-policy line remains small relative to the decorative headlines. This is a hierarchy refinement, not a blocker.
7. **Contact — healthy.** Strong yellow action, readable close, and orderly contact information. The prototype disclosure correctly identifies conceptual artwork and incomplete migration/video integration. No observed collision in the supplied phone/desktop states.
8. **Reduced motion — previously inspected static state healthy; fresh behavior not certified.** Prior inspected reduced-motion compositions showed complete headline and CTA. This visual review does not certify live animation timing, all reduced-motion transitions, or final video quality.

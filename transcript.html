NILKEEPR / DOCUMENT LIBRARY

The school overview.
In their words.

Athletic director, imagine this happens at your school today.

A sixteen-year-old student, not a five-star recruit,

just a kid who's good at social media,

gets offered seven hundred dollars by a local business for a few posts.

They take the money, make the posts, and everyone moves on.

Two years later, that student is preparing to become a college athlete.

Now, their name, image, and likeness,

or NIL history, can matter.

Under current NCAA rules, qualifying incoming prospects

have NIL disclosure and documentation requirements.

Suddenly, somebody needs answers.

Who paid them? Where's the agreement?

What did they agree to? What rights did they give away?

And can anyone produce the documentation?

That's the hidden NIL problem.

Students can begin creating an NIL history

years before anyone knows they're going to become college athletes.

But there's another question schools need to consider.

What happens when it's the parent or their attorney asking those questions?

If a student suffers financial harm, an eligibility issue,

unauthorized use of their NIL, or a contract dispute,

the school may have to explain what policies,

education, records, and safeguards it had in place.

Whether that creates legal liability depends on the facts and applicable law.

But having no documented system can leave the school

in a much more difficult position when responding to a complaint,

investigation, or claim.

So ask yourself, could your school demonstrate today

what it did to educate and protect its student-athletes?

That's why NILKEEPr exists.

The software does the heavy lifting.

NILKEEPr helps track, monitor, document, and report

NIL activity in one centralized system

without requiring your school to build an NIL department

or add staff simply to manage the process.

We start the NIL record in high school,

giving your athletic department visibility and families a documented

history before college comes calling.

Don't wait until you're answering these questions across the table

from a parent or an attorney.

Contact NILKEEPr today and establish your pilot program

before there's a problem.

One system, one dashboard, one student-athlete record.

Your name, image, likeness is your asset.

NILKEEPr, yours to keep.

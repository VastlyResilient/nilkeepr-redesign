About NILKEEPR

One ecosystem.
Distinct responsibilities.

NILKEEPR connects athletes, agents, brands, schools, and families across the name, image, and likeness landscape.

A transparent starting point

Talent meets
opportunity.

NILKEEPR’s mission is to create a transparent, compliant, and empowering NIL ecosystem. Its stated vision brings athlete development, representation, brand partnerships, institutional oversight, and parental protection into one platform.

The school-compliance service has a deliberately separate role: disclosure, documentation, and read-only oversight. It does not facilitate or negotiate NIL deals.

Student-athletes

AlphaMind™ assessment, sport-specific skills evaluation, media and brand analysis, marketplace access, deal management, and performance analytics.

Contact the student-athletes team

Agents

A three-tier certification pathway: Foundation covers NIL fundamentals; Professional covers negotiation and athlete management; Expert serves experienced representatives. Client portfolios, commission tracking, and compliance tools support the wider agent program.

Contact the agents team

Brand partners

Athlete discovery, campaign management, deal workflow, and performance metrics support brand partnerships in the wider NILKEEPR ecosystem.

Contact the brand partners team

Schools & districts

Roster management, disclosure tracking, compliance visibility, reports, and exports support institutional oversight.

Contact the schools & districts team

Parents & guardians

Consent and guardian controls provide a defined way to review minor athletes’ activities and information access.

Contact the parents & guardians team

Wider platform capabilities

NILKEEPR describes a marketplace for athlete and brand opportunities; Stripe Connect-powered payments; digital portal agreements with PDF audit trails, SHA-256 verification, and signature capture; and guardian approval and disclosure workflows. These are company-described features of the wider platform, not functions provided by this informational website.

The company identifies Protected Harbor as its sole technology and hosting vendor. Its published location is Greenwich, Connecticut 06830.

California Privacy Notice (effective January 1, 2025)

In the preceding 12 months we collected identifiers/contact details, commercial and transaction information, internet and device activity, professional/education/athletic profile information, payment information, and limited sensitive personal information such as account credentials and precise location where needed. Sources include consumers, guardians, schools, organizations, service providers, and Platform interactions. Purposes include providing and securing services, payments, communications, opportunity matching, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and service improvement.

We disclose these categories to hosting, payment, communications, identity verification, analytics, professional, and other service providers, to parties consumers choose, and as required by law. We do not sell personal information for money. We may share identifiers and online activity for cross-context behavioral advertising only where permitted and subject to Do Not Sell/Share controls. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics or for purposes beyond providing the requested service.

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes collected, legal obligations, safety, dispute resolution, and account/deal records, then delete or de-identify it. Minors receive age-appropriate protections and verified guardian involvement where required; we do not knowingly sell or share a minor's information.

California consumers may request access, portability, correction, deletion, limitation of sensitive-information use, and may use an authorized agent. We verify requests using reasonable account or identity information, respond within 45 days, may extend once by 45 days with notice, explain denials and appeal instructions, and do not discriminate for exercising rights. Contact legal support through the Privacy Request Center or legal@nilkeepr.com.

Optional campaign measurement

Optional campaign measurement links campaign emails to approved public pages, estimated active time, and successful demo bookings, non-athlete signups, eligible platform purchases and saved forms. Full website-request IP addresses are encrypted and kept for up to 30 days; other optional measurement is kept for up to 90 days. IPs never identify visitors. Athletes and minors are excluded. Rejecting or withdrawing does not affect site services.

This separate purpose is disabled until privacy and security review is approved. It requires an explicit choice in Cookie Settings, adult non-athlete eligibility and current server-held consent; ordinary analytics or marketing consent alone does not enable it. “Accept All” does not opt you into this purpose.

Public page names, session times and four server-confirmed conversion categories may be associated with an eligible campaign link. Forwarded links do not establish recipient identity. Website IPs are not email-provider or proxy IPs and can reflect a VPN or shared connection. Authorized administrators see masked website IPs; full-IP disclosure requires an approved, audited purpose.

No private routes, raw query strings, authentication or capability tokens, form contents, payment details, precise location, fingerprinting, cross-device matching or pre-consent browsing are collected. Athletes are excluded at every age, even with parental permission. Missing or conflicting identity is not tracked.

Withdraw in Cookie Settings or use the existing privacy opt-out or account deletion process. Collection stops on the next request; optional associations and data are removed. Original bookings, transactions, security, legal and consent evidence keep their separate retention policies. Estimated active time and campaign-link association are not proof of attention or causation.

Privacy Policy

Effective: July 9, 2026 (v3.1)

Provider: NILKEEPr inc.

Privacy Contact: legal@nilkeepr.net

1. Introduction

NILKEEPr inc. ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our NIL (Name, Image, and Likeness) platform, including our website, mobile applications, and related services (collectively, the "Platform").

By using our Platform, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the Platform.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

• Full name, email address, phone number, and mailing address

• Date of birth and age verification data

• Professional credentials, licenses, and certifications (for agents)

• Payment and billing information (processed securely via Stripe)

• Government-issued identification (when required for verification or payout onboarding)

Athletic & NIL Data

• Sport, position, school/team affiliation, and performance statistics

• Social media handles, follower counts, and engagement metrics

• NIL valuation scores and market assessments

• Brand partnership history and deal information

• NCAA eligibility status and compliance records

AI, Voice & Communications Data

• Chat conversations with our AI assistants (message content and metadata)

• Voice call recordings and transcriptions from calls with our AI voice agents and support lines

• SMS messages exchanged with the Platform

• Email engagement data (delivery, opens, and clicks on messages we send)

• Appointment booking details you share during AI conversations

Usage & Technical Data

• IP address, browser type, device information, and operating system

• Pages visited, features used, and time spent on the Platform

• Referral sources and click-through data

• Cookies and similar tracking technologies

3. How We Use Your Information

• NIL Services: To calculate NIL valuations, match athletes with brand opportunities, and facilitate NIL deals

• AI Services: To operate AI chat and voice assistants, personalize responses using your account context, and improve safety and quality (see the AI disclosure in our Terms)

• Platform Operations: To provide, maintain, and improve our services

• Payments: To process subscriptions, deal escrow, and payouts through Stripe and Stripe Connect

• Compliance: To verify NCAA eligibility, state licensing requirements, and regulatory compliance

• Communications: To send service updates, deal notifications, and marketing materials (with consent; opt out anytime)

• Analytics: To understand usage patterns and improve user experience

• Security: To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access

• Legal: To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms of service

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We may share your information with:

• Brand Partners: Athlete profiles may be shared with brands seeking NIL partnerships (athletes control visibility settings)

• Agents: Athletes who connect with agents consent to sharing relevant profile data

• Schools & Compliance Officers: NIL deal information may be shared with institutional compliance departments as required

• Parents & Guardians: For athletes under 18, the verified parent or guardian has access to the minor's NIL activity, deals, and account data

• Service Providers: Third-party vendors who process data on our behalf, including Stripe (payments and payouts), Twilio (SMS and voice calls), SendGrid (email delivery), ElevenLabs (AI voice generation), Anthropic (AI language models), and our hosting and analytics providers. These providers are bound by contractual obligations to protect your data

• Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or governmental authority

• Business Transfers: In connection with mergers, acquisitions, or sale of assets

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

Product analytics is first-party and optional. We use normalized pages, short-lived anonymous session keys, time spent, and a fixed list of meaningful product events to improve the Platform. We do not use this stream for session replay, advertising profiles, precise location, device fingerprints, form text, education records, grades, eligibility, compliance, deal, or payment details.

5. Data Security

• All data transmission is encrypted using TLS/SSL protocols

• Passwords are hashed using industry-standard bcrypt encryption

• Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only

• Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments are conducted

• Payment information is processed through PCI-DSS compliant services (Stripe)

• We maintain incident response procedures for potential data breaches

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to:

• Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you

• Correction: Update or correct inaccurate personal information

• Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)

• Portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format

• Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails via the link in any message, and stop SMS messages by replying STOP

• Marketing email preferences and unsubscribe requests are described in our Email Outreach Policy.

• Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data in certain circumstances

To exercise these rights, visit the Privacy Request Center in your account settings or contact us at legal@nilkeepr.net. From your settings you can download a full export of your data, submit access, correction, or deletion requests, and opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data.

7. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

• Essential Cookies: Required for platform functionality (authentication, security)

• Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with the Platform

• Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect Platform functionality.

8. Data Retention

• Active account data is retained for the duration of your account

• Transaction records are retained for 7 years for tax and legal compliance

• AI chat transcripts and voice call recordings are retained only as long as needed for quality, safety, and compliance purposes, then deleted or anonymized

• Upon account deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days (except where legally required)

• Optional product-analytics and retargeting rows are deleted after 90 days; essential security, consent, privacy, and legal audit records follow separate retention rules

9. Minor Athletes (COPPA)

For athletes under 18 years of age, we require verified guardian consent before collecting personal information. Guardian accounts, managed through our Parent Portal, can view and manage minor athlete profiles, must approve all NIL deals and agent connections, and receive payouts on the minor's behalf. For children under 13, optional product analytics and retargeting are not collected unless a current, verifiable parental consent record expressly covers that use. Parents can withdraw that permission and request review or deletion of their child's information at any time by contacting legal@nilkeepr.net. Essential security, consent, and legal records remain separate.

10. US State Privacy Rights (CCPA and others)

Residents of California (CCPA/CPRA) and other states with comprehensive privacy laws (including Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, and Virginia) have additional rights:

• Right to know what personal information is collected, used, and shared

• Right to delete personal information held by us

• Right to correct inaccurate personal information

• Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell personal data)

• Right to limit use of sensitive personal information

• Right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

To exercise these rights, use the Privacy Request Center in your account settings or email legal@nilkeepr.net. We will verify your identity before fulfilling the request and respond within 45 days (or sooner where your state's law requires it).

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top. For significant changes, we will provide additional notice via email or through the Platform. Your continued use of the Platform after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

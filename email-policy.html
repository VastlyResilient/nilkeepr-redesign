RECIPIENT-FIRST COMMUNICATIONS

Email Communications & Outreach Policy

NILKEEPr inc. · Updated 2026-10-02

Marketing email is optional. We separate promotional outreach from messages needed to provide a service or protect your account.

01

Optional marketing email

We send marketing email only when you have separately and affirmatively asked NILKEEPr inc. to send you email marketing. Providing an email address, creating an account, submitting a form, accepting our Terms, using the platform, or having an address in a public or imported directory does not count as that permission. Marketing consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase.

02

What counts as marketing

Marketing includes promotional messages about our services, educational resources, demos, consultations, or opportunities. We do not treat a promotional message as a service notice just because it is sent to a customer or includes account-related information.

03

Service and account messages

We may still send messages needed to provide a service you requested or maintain your account, such as security alerts, receipts, deal or appointment updates, legal notices, and replies to support requests. We keep these messages focused on their purpose; an unsubscribe from marketing does not subscribe you to marketing or stop necessary service communications.

04

Unsubscribe and preferences

Every marketing email includes a clear way to unsubscribe without signing in or paying a fee. You may also manage or withdraw your marketing email choice at any time. We apply the request promptly and before another marketing send, and no later than the period required by law. Marketing preferences are separate from support-notification choices and from transactional delivery.

05

Privacy and young people

We do not use a parent or guardian’s general platform permission as permission to send marketing email to a young person. We exclude known minors and recipients whose age or permission cannot be verified from promotional outreach. Required safeguards for children and their information remain in place.

06

Email delivery

We use email service providers to deliver messages and honor email preferences. Those providers may process delivery, unsubscribe, complaint, and bounce information for these purposes. We do not sell or transfer an address on an opt-out list for marketing.

Data & consent

Visibility is useful.
Boundaries are essential.

A parent-consented, student-reported environment with read-only access for schools.

Who controls the dataWhat schools seeConsent & safeguards

The student remains the source

Student-athletes self-submit academic and NIL disclosures. Parents or guardians provide consent for minors. Schools view compliance-relevant information; they do not input, edit, or control data.

Limited by design

The school view includes school name and class year, reported academic eligibility status, NIL disclosure summaries, consent status, disclosure timestamps, and audit history. Exact compensation figures are not displayed.

Full dates of birth, parent contact information, private communications, financial account details, and non-compliance or marketing data are excluded.

Child privacy & consent

The source requires parent or guardian consent for athletes under 18. Consent is recorded, timestamped, and revocable. Parents can review the information made available to schools.

FERPA-aligned architecture

NILKEEPR describes voluntary student submissions, access under legitimate educational interest, auditable school access, and an architecture that does not replace or duplicate school education-record systems. It describes its platform as FERPA-aligned and COPPA-compliant. These are company representations, not independent verification of an institution’s legal compliance.

Auditability & security

NILKEEPR states that school access is logged and timestamped, data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and breach-notification protocols are in place. It also states that school-compliance data is not sold, marketed, or shared externally. A Student Data Privacy Addendum is available on request; review the full privacy policy and contractual terms for their applicable scope and exceptions.

Communications Consent

NILKEEPr inc. — version 1.0

This page explains how NILKEEPr inc. communicates with people who ask about our platform, services, consultations, or NIL opportunities. It is also our public disclosure for SMS and email communications.

Text messages and calls

If you separately and affirmatively opt in, NILKEEPr inc. may send SMS messages and make calls, including calls or messages made with automated technology or AI voice agents, about requested demos, consultations, platform information, service updates, and, where specifically authorized, marketing offers. Message frequency varies and is not guaranteed.

Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any product or service.

Reply STOP to any text to opt out of SMS messages. Reply HELP for help. You can also contact support@nilportal.net or call 1-877-419-7715. Opting out of marketing SMS does not stop essential transactional or account messages where applicable.

Email communications

With separate affirmative consent, we may send marketing email about NILKEEPr inc. services, educational resources, demos, consultations, and opportunities. Frequency varies. Each marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing from marketing email does not stop transactional, security, legal, or service-related messages.

Our Email Outreach Policy explains how we identify marketing email, handle unsubscribe requests, and protect recipient preferences.

Your choices and our terms

Email marketing consent and SMS consent are separate choices. We do not infer consent from submitting a form, providing a phone number, accepting the Terms, or continuing to use the platform. Marketing consent is never a condition of purchase. You may withdraw either choice at any time.

Read our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions. Questions about communications can be sent to support@nilportal.net.

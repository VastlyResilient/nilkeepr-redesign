HOW IT CONNECTS

One record.
Three distinct roles.

The information changes hands.
The responsibilities stay clear.

The athlete

Student-athletes self-submit NIL activity and reported academic eligibility information. The student remains the source of truth.

The parent

For minors, guardian consent is recorded, timestamped, and revocable. Parents can review what schools can access.

The school

Administrators view relevant information in a read-only capacity. They do not create, edit, or control student records.

Understand data & consent
Visible.

Read-only information, provided by the athlete and supported by consent.

  • School name and class year
  • Reported academic eligibility status
  • NIL disclosure summaries, without exact dollar amounts
  • Active or expired consent status
  • Disclosure timestamps and audit history

A DEFINED ROLE

Observe. Document.
Stay independent of the deal.

No deal negotiation, athlete valuation, NIL commissions, or recruiting influence. No replacement for the school’s SIS, gradebook, or eligibility authority.

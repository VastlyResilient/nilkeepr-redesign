Documentation with context

The company describes immutable, timestamped audit logs, logged school access, encrypted data in transit and at rest, breach-notification protocols, and reports prepared for state associations or court inquiries. These are NILKEEPR’s stated platform features, not independent certifications or guarantees of an eligibility or legal outcome.

Disclosures originate with student-athletes; consent is required for minors. Schools view relevant information but do not create or maintain the athlete’s education records through this platform.

Oversight without deal involvement

  • No negotiation of NIL deals.
  • No approval or denial of opportunities.
  • No athlete valuations or NIL commissions.
  • No influence on recruiting decisions.
  • No replacement for a school’s SIS, gradebook, or eligibility authority.

The service is intended to support transparency and compliance in the state of operation. Ask the school team about your institution’s specific requirements.

Institutional licensing

Individual-school licensing includes dashboard access, disclosure monitoring, documentation, and onboarding. District master agreements add a centralized view, bulk reporting, and priority implementation and training. The source describes no per-athlete fees, no usage-based pricing, and no NIL commissions.

Public pricing is not published. Existing account holders can access their school account; new institutions can request a demonstration and tailored plan details.
